What do we treat?

Diagnosis and follow-up of chest diseases

Using appropriate lab tests, imaging and pulmonary function tests.

Allergy consultation

Assessment and treatment of allergy and seasonal respiratory conditions.

Tuberculosis (TB) consultation

Diagnosis and follow-up of pulmonary tuberculosis (TB).

Chronic chest disease follow-up

Follow-up of chronic conditions such as asthma and chronic bronchitis.

Sleep disorder diagnosis

Medical care for children from 6 months of age.

Asthma treatment

Personalised treatment plans to control asthma and prevent attacks.

Chronic lung infection treatment

Treating chronic and recurrent lung infections to relieve symptoms and improve breathing.

COPD treatment

Treating chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) to improve breathing and quality of life.

Acute and chronic bronchitis

Treating bronchitis to reduce symptoms and improve breathing.

Respiratory failure and home oxygen

Follow-up of critical cases that need respiratory support.

Blood tests for chest diseases

Blood tests to diagnose respiratory diseases and lung infections.

Shortness of breath assessment

Assessing the causes of chronic or sudden shortness of breath to determine the right treatment.

Viral and bacterial pneumonia

Treating pneumonia caused by viruses and bacteria.

Latent tuberculosis (TB)

Identifying and treating latent TB to prevent the disease from developing.

Follow-up after lung surgery

Following patients after lung surgery to ensure full recovery.
