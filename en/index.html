About the doctor

Dr. Seham Salem — Consultant in Chest Diseases, Allergy and Tuberculosis (TB)

Holds a Master's degree in Chest Diseases and Tuberculosis (TB) from Cairo University and the Egyptian Fellowship in Chest Diseases, and has worked in intensive care units and several of the largest chest hospitals.

  • More than 20 years of experience in chest diseases and tuberculosis (TB)
  • Abbassia Chest Hospitals, the Health Insurance Hospital in Nasr City, and Al-Nasr Specialized Hospital (formerly Rabaa El-Adawia)
  • More than 4 years of experience in the intensive care unit
  • Master's in Chest Diseases and Tuberculosis (TB) — Kasr Al-Ainy, Cairo University
  • Egyptian Fellowship in Chest Diseases
